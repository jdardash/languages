# Portuguese

**The book:** *Colloquial Portuguese of Brazil* (Routledge Colloquial series).
The most complete single self-study course for Brazilian Portuguese; all audio free
at https://routledgetextbooks.com/textbooks/colloquial — if European Portuguese ever
matters more, the sister volume *Colloquial Portuguese* covers it.

**Free and legal alternative:** FSI Portuguese Programmatic Course (public domain,
Brazilian): https://www.livelingua.com

**First move:** decide Brazilian vs European once (Brazilian is the default: more
media, more speakers), then Colloquial unit 1 with the audio.

## Path to fluency (learn everything here → B2/C1)

1. **Foundation:** Colloquial Portuguese of Brazil, all units with audio.
2. **Grammar reference as needed:** *Modern Brazilian Portuguese Grammar* (Routledge)
   — the lookup book, not a read-through.
3. **Input engine (daily):**
   - Fala Gringo — intermediate-learner podcast, slow natural Brazilian:
     https://falagringo.com
   - Speaking Brazilian (pronunciation-focused lessons):
     https://www.youtube.com/@SpeakingBrazilian
   - Graded reading: *Short Stories in Brazilian Portuguese* (Olly Richards).
4. **Speaking:** tutor on https://www.italki.com — prioritize pronunciation
   correction early; the Spanish discount makes grammar cheap but the sounds are the
   real work (nasal vowels, reduced endings).
5. **Native transition:** Brazilian Netflix, Porta dos Fundos sketches, Brazilian
   music with lyrics.

**Milestone that means "fluent":** Celpe-Bras intermediate tasks feel doable; you can
follow rapid carioca/paulista speech and answer in kind.
