# Spanish

**The book:** *Complete Spanish Step-by-Step* — Barbara Bregstein (McGraw-Hill).
The modern self-study consensus: full A1-B1 grammar progression with exercises, in one
volume. Runner-up (and a classic worth knowing): *Madrigal's Magic Key to Spanish* —
brilliant on cognates, dated in format.

**Free and legal alternatives:** Language Transfer "Complete Spanish" (free audio
course — the best free start in any language): https://www.languagetransfer.org
FSI Spanish Programmatic Course (public domain): https://www.livelingua.com

**First move:** Language Transfer tracks 1-10 in a week, then Bregstein Part 1 with
every exercise done on paper.

## Path to fluency (learn everything here → B2/C1)

1. **Foundation:** Language Transfer complete (90 tracks) + Bregstein, every exercise.
2. **Input engine (daily, start immediately — Spanish rewards it fastest):**
   - Dreaming Spanish — the largest comprehensible-input library in any language,
     graded from superbeginner to advanced: https://www.dreamingspanish.com
   - News in Slow Spanish: https://www.newsinslowspanish.com
   - Graded reading: *Short Stories in Spanish* (Olly Richards), then Spanish news.
3. **Speaking:** 1-2 tutor sessions/week on https://www.italki.com from Bregstein's
   halfway point; Spanish is everywhere in California — use it weekly in the wild.
4. **Native transition:** Radio Ambulante (narrative podcast, transcripts):
   https://radioambulante.org — plus any Netflix series in Spanish with Spanish subs.

**Milestone that means "fluent":** DELE B2 tasks feel doable; a full evening of
conversation in Spanish leaves you tired but never stuck.

**Note:** finish Spanish to B2 before starting Portuguese or French — the discount
compounds in that order.
