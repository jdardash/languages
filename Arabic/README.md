# Arabic

**The book:** *Mastering Arabic 1* — Jane Wightwick & Mahmoud Gaafar.
The consensus pick for self-learners: teaches the script from zero and reaches a
solid Modern Standard Arabic (MSA) base, friendlier than the classroom-oriented
*Al-Kitaab* series. If the script alone feels like the wall, *Alif Baa* is the
dedicated script-and-sounds primer.

**Free and legal alternatives:** Language Transfer "Intro to Arabic" (free audio,
Egyptian): https://www.languagetransfer.org — FSI Arabic courses (public domain):
https://www.livelingua.com

**First move:** the alphabet in Mastering Arabic ch. 1-2 until you can sound out any
word slowly — everything else waits on the script.

## Path to fluency (learn everything here → B2/C1)

Arabic fluency is a two-track project: MSA (reading, news, formal) plus one spoken
dialect (conversation). Neither alone is "fluent."

1. **Foundation (MSA):** Mastering Arabic 1, then *Mastering Arabic 2*.
2. **Dialect (pick once, at the end of book 1):**
   - Egyptian (most media): Language Transfer Intro + the *Kallimni 'Arabi* series
     (AUC Press).
   - Levantine (Syria/Lebanon/Jordan/Palestine): *Colloquial Levantine Arabic*
     (Routledge).
3. **Input engine:**
   - Al Jazeera's learner platform — graded MSA news with exercises:
     https://learning.aljazeera.net
   - Easy Arabic (street interviews across dialects):
     https://www.youtube.com/@EasyArabic
4. **Speaking:** tutor on https://www.italki.com in your chosen dialect from the
   start of track 2 — dialect lives only in conversation.
5. **Native transition:** Arab TV drama in your dialect with Arabic subs; MSA news
   broadcasts read along.

**Milestone that means "fluent":** you follow Al Jazeera headlines cold (MSA) and
hold a relaxed 30-minute conversation with a native speaker in your dialect. FSI
Category V — this is a multi-year track; budget honestly.
