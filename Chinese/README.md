# Chinese (Mandarin)

**The book:** *Integrated Chinese, Volume 1* (4th ed.) — Liu, Yao, et al.
The standard first-year university text and the safest complete base: characters,
grammar, dialogues, workbook. Finish Volume 1 with understanding and you hold a real
HSK 2-3 foundation.

**Free and legal alternative:** FSI Standard Chinese: A Modular Approach (public
domain, superb audio drilling): https://www.livelingua.com

**First move:** pinyin and the four tones drilled with audio until reliable (1-2
weeks) — tones learned wrong at the start are the classic unfixable error — then
Integrated Chinese lesson 1 with characters in Anki from day one.

## Path to fluency (learn everything here → B2/C1, ~HSK 5-6)

1. **Foundation:** Integrated Chinese Volumes 1-4 (the 4th ed. series covers two
   full university years → solid B1).
2. **Tools from day one:** Pleco (the dictionary app, non-negotiable):
   https://www.pleco.com — Anki for characters/vocab, always in context, never
   isolated character grinding.
3. **Reading engine (start after Vol. 1):**
   - Du Chinese — graded reader app: https://www.duchinese.net
   - The Chairman's Bao — graded news: https://www.thechairmansbao.com
   - Mandarin Companion — graded novels in print.
4. **Listening engine:**
   - Teatime Chinese — beginner/intermediate podcast: https://www.teatimechinese.com
   - Comprehensible Chinese (graded video):
     https://www.youtube.com/@ComprehensibleChinese
5. **Speaking:** tutor on https://www.italki.com from Vol. 2 — tones survive only
   with live correction.
6. **Native transition:** Chinese TV with Chinese subtitles (subtitles are universal
   in Chinese media — use them as training wheels).

**Milestone that means "fluent":** HSK 5 passable; you follow a Chinese dinner
conversation and read a news article with Pleco taps only for rare words. FSI
Category V — the longest road in this folder along with Japanese and Arabic.
