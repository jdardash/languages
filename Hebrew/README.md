# Hebrew

**The book:** *Hebrew from Scratch, Part 1* (עברית מן ההתחלה) — Chayat, Israeli &
Yerushalmi. The standard Israeli ulpan textbook and the consensus base for Modern
Hebrew; immersive (Hebrew-forward) by design. If you want an English-scaffolded
companion, *The Routledge Introductory Course in Modern Hebrew* is the alternative.

**Free and legal alternative:** FSI Hebrew Basic Course (public domain):
https://www.livelingua.com

**First move:** the alef-bet plus vowel points until you can sound out any pointed
word, then Hebrew from Scratch unit 1.

## Path to fluency (learn everything here → B2/C1)

1. **Foundation:** Hebrew from Scratch Part 1, then *Part 2* (the pair is the full
   ulpan Aleph-Bet sequence → solid B1).
2. **Input engine:**
   - Streetwise Hebrew — TLV1's podcast on real spoken Israeli Hebrew, slang
     included: https://tlv1.fm/streetwisehebrew
   - Israeli TV with subtitles (Shtisel, Fauda), rewatched targeting listening.
   - News in easier Hebrew, read aloud, as reading practice once unpointed text
     starts to click.
3. **Speaking:** tutor on https://www.italki.com — Israeli Hebrew is direct and
   fast; live conversation early is the only way to keep up with it.
4. **Native transition:** unpointed text (newspapers, subtitles) as the standing
   reading medium — pointed text is training wheels, and coming off them is a
   deliberate milestone, not an accident.

**Milestone that means "fluent":** you read an unpointed news article and hold a
rapid-fire conversation with an Israeli without asking them to slow down twice.

**Scope note:** this is Modern Israeli Hebrew. Biblical Hebrew (if the faith shelf
ever pulls that way) is a separate project — Pratico & Van Pelt, *Basics of
Biblical Hebrew*.
