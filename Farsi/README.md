# Farsi (Persian)

**The book:** *Complete Modern Persian* — Narguess Farzad (Teach Yourself).
The standard self-study course for Iranian Persian: script from zero, graded
dialogues, audio, to about A2-B1. Persian grammar is famously gentle (no gender, no
cases, regular verbs); the script and the formal/colloquial split are the real work.

**Free and legal alternative:** DLI / Peace Corps Persian materials (public domain):
https://www.livelingua.com

**First move:** the Perso-Arabic script in the book's introduction until you can
sound out words, then unit 1 with audio.

## Path to fluency (learn everything here → B2/C1)

1. **Foundation:** Farzad cover to cover, both registers (formal + colloquial
   Tehrani) as the book presents them.
2. **Structured continuation:** Chai and Conversation — the standard Persian
   audio course, beginner through advanced, colloquial-first:
   https://www.chaiandconversation.com
3. **Input engine:**
   - Easy Persian — long-running free lesson site: https://easypersian.com
   - BBC Persian for news exposure: https://www.bbc.com/persian
   - Iranian cinema (Farhadi's films — A Separation, The Salesman) with subtitles,
     rewatched without.
4. **Speaking:** tutor on https://www.italki.com — insist on colloquial Tehrani, not
   book-formal; the spoken/written gap is Persian's one real trap.
5. **Native transition:** Persian podcasts and music (lyrics are poetry-heavy — a
   feature, given the literature: Hafez and Rumi in the original are this folder's
   summit).

**Milestone that means "fluent":** you follow a Tehrani family conversation at
natural speed and can read a newspaper article slowly without transliteration.
