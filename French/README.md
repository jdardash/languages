# French

**The book:** *Assimil — New French with Ease* — Anthony Bulger (English-base edition).
The single most respected self-study course in any language: 113 short dialogue
lessons with audio, passive wave then active wave, ending around B1-B2 comprehension.
Runner-up: *Complete French* (Teach Yourself) if you prefer a conventional textbook.

**Free and legal alternative:** FSI French Basic Course (public domain):
https://www.livelingua.com — dry but thorough.

**First move:** Assimil lesson 1, out loud, with the audio — one lesson a day, every
day; the method only works as a daily rhythm.

## Path to fluency (learn everything here → B2/C1)

1. **Foundation:** Assimil complete — both waves (passive then active). Done honestly
   this alone reaches B1-B2 comprehension.
2. **Input engine (start around Assimil lesson 50):**
   - InnerFrench — the standard intermediate podcast + courses, slow natural French
     with transcripts: https://innerfrench.com
   - Journal en français facile — RFI's daily news in simplified French:
     https://francaisfacile.rfi.fr
   - Easy French (street interviews): https://www.youtube.com/@EasyFrench
   - Graded reading: *Short Stories in French* (Olly Richards), then Le Petit Prince,
     then real novels.
3. **Speaking:** 1-2 tutor sessions/week on https://www.italki.com during Assimil's
   active wave — French listening (liaisons, reductions) needs live exposure.
4. **Native transition:** French podcasts (Transfert), films with French subtitles.

**Milestone that means "fluent":** DELF B2 tasks feel doable; you follow a French
dinner-table conversation and contribute without rehearsing sentences in your head.
